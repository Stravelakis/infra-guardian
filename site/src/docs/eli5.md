## infra-guardian, like you're five

You have a toy city made of blocks: websites and helpers that you built. Some blocks hold up other blocks. If you pull the wrong one, things fall down.

infra-guardian is a very careful grown-up who stands next to your city. Before it touches any block, it takes a photo so you can put it back. Then it tells you what it wants to do, in simple words. It only moves one block, and only when you say "yes".

If you say "no", it remembers and does not keep asking.
