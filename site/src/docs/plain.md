## infra-guardian in plain English

You build real things (websites, AI tools, a CRM) but you are not a coder, and one wrong click can break something you cannot fix. infra-guardian is the safety net.

It keeps an eye on your servers and services, tells you in plain words what is going on, and **never changes anything without backing it up and asking you first.**

**The one promise:** back up, preview, ask, change one thing, write it down.

- **Watches** that your services are alive.
- **Explains** with no jargon. A new word goes into a glossary.
- **Backs up first.** It checks the copy is perfect before it touches anything.
- **Asks first.** One change at a time, and it waits for your yes.
- **Does not nag.** Say no once and it will not ask again until something changes.
- **Learns your setup.** It keeps simple notes on every tool you use.

**Is it for you?** Yes, if you self-host apps but do not write code, and you want a plain-English explanation before anything changes. Probably not, if you are an experienced sysadmin who wants full manual control with no guardrails.

You do not need any secret keys to use the basics. Keys are only for optional extras such as phone alerts.
